# Shipping

## Orders

Amazon ships every order. Support cannot see or change Amazon deliveries.

## Replacement parts

Replacement parts and hardware kits ship free of charge from our warehouse. They leave within 2 business days and arrive in 3 to 7 business days. The customer gets a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves.

## Countries

We sell in every country where our products are listed on Amazon. Replacement parts ship to the same address as the original order.
