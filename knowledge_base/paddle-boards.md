# Paddle boards

## Sizes

- 10'6" All-round: riders up to 85 kg. Best for beginners and calm water.
- 11' All-round: riders up to 110 kg. The most stable board, good for two light riders.
- 12'6" Touring: riders up to 130 kg. Faster and longer.

A rider between two sizes should take the larger board.

## What is in the box

Every board comes with:

- 3-piece adjustable paddle (PB-PADDLE)
- Double-action hand pump with gauge (PB-PUMP)
- Removable center fin (PB-FIN)
- Coiled leash (PB-LEASH)
- Valve wrench (PB-WRENCH)
- Carry bag (PB-BAG)

## Inflation

Inflate the board to 12 to 15 PSI. Below 12 PSI the board bends in the middle. Losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight is normal.

## Valve check for slow leaks

1. Inflate the board to 15 PSI.
2. Spray soapy water on the valve.
3. Turn the valve core a quarter turn clockwise with the valve wrench.
4. Spray the valve again.
5. Check the pressure after 24 hours.

If the board still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, ask for the order number, the board size, and how many days since it arrived.

## Problems and what happens next

A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one.

- Missing accessory: we ship the part. No return needed.
- Slow leak: do the valve check first.
- Still leaking after the valve check, within 30 days of delivery: a new board.
- Still leaking after the valve check, more than 30 days after delivery: a patch kit.
- Split seam: always goes to a colleague.

To arrange anything, ask for the order number, the board size, and a short description of the problem.
