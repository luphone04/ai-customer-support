# Shelving

## Models and finishes

Every model comes in four finishes: natural oak, white, black, and walnut.

- HP-S3: 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high
- HP-S5: 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high
- HP-C4: 2x2 cube unit, 77 cm high
- HP-C9: 3x3 cube unit, 112 cm high

The model code is printed on the assembly manual and on the box label.

## Parts lists

A panel part number is the model code plus the panel number, for example HP-S3-04.

HP-S3: 01 left side, 02 right side, 03 top, 04 shelf, 05 bottom, 06 back panel.

HP-S5: 01 left side, 02 right side, 03 top, 04 to 06 shelves, 07 bottom, 08 back panel, 09 kick plate.

HP-C4: 01 top, 02 bottom, 03 left side, 04 right side, 05 center divider, 06 shelf, 07 back panel.

HP-C9: 01 top, 02 bottom, 03 left side, 04 right side, 05 and 06 dividers, 07 and 08 shelves, 09 back panel.

Hardware kits hold all cam locks, dowels, screws, and the wall anchor. HK-S fits the S models (HP-S3, HP-S5). HK-C fits the C models (HP-C4, HP-C9).

## Assembly tips

1. Lay the panels out on the box or a rug so they do not scratch.
2. The arrow on each cam lock points at the cam bolt hole. Turn it clockwise until it stops, about half a turn.
3. The back panel goes in with the finished side facing into the shelf.
4. Tall units (HP-S5, HP-C9) must be fixed to the wall with the included anchor.

Assembly takes 30 to 60 minutes with one person. A cordless screwdriver on a low setting is fine.

## Problems and what happens next

- Missing screws or hardware: the hardware kit for the model.
- 1 or 2 damaged panels: those panels, shipped by part number.
- 3 or more damaged panels, or a damaged frame: a full replacement.
- Wrong finish: an exchange.

To arrange anything, ask for the order number, the model, and the part numbers of the damaged panels. A photo helps.
