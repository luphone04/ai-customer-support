# Returns and warranty

## Returns

Returns go through Amazon. The customer starts a return within 30 days of delivery in Your Orders on Amazon. Amazon sends the return label and handles the refund. Support cannot start, speed up, or approve returns or refunds.

## Warranty

- Paddle boards: 2 years on the board, seams, and valve. 1 year on the paddle, pump, fin, and leash.
- Shelving: 2 years on panels and hardware.

The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover damage from use, such as a puncture from a rock.

## Other products

All other products follow the same rules: 30-day returns through Amazon and a 2-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
